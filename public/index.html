Core features

Designed around understanding and verification.

Jargon Decoder

Turn complicated medical and billing terms into short, plain-language explanations.

Document Match

Compare related information across healthcare documents instead of analyzing every document separately.

Show Source

Important explanations can point users back to the information that appeared in the original document.

Consistency Check

Highlight possible differences between information found across bills, EOBs, and other healthcare paperwork.
